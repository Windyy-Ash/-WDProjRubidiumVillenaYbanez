Act II — Omnipresence Over Mortals

The Traveler sails to Inazuma under the Vision Hunt Decree, where visions are confiscated in the name of eternity. Joining the resistance led by Sangonomiya Kokomi and allies like Thoma and Yoimiya, the Traveler challenges the Raiden Shogun’s enforcement of the decree.

With Ei confronting her own ideals of eternity, the decree is lifted and the Shogun chooses to guide Inazuma forward rather than freeze it in the past.
