Meats & Seafood Wild Gathered Processed or Bought

Ingredients || How to get it

Meats & Seafood

Raw Meat - Found in the wild. Sold by Ashpazi, Azalai, and Draff. Reward from Expeditions. Output from Processing.
Bird Egg - Found in the wild. Sold by Ashpazi, Draff, and Pahsiv. Reward from Expeditions.
Fowl - Found in the wild. Sold by Ashpazi, Draff, and Pahsiv. Reward from Expeditions.
Fish - Dropped by Basses. Sold by Azalai, Bolai, Butrus, Chef Mao, Hinterman, Lambad, Obata, Pahsiv, Pam, Shimura Kanbei, Uncle Gao, and Uncle Sun. Processing.
Crab - Found near lakes, river banks, and beaches. Sold by Butrus, Chef Mao, Hinterman, Lambad, Obata, Pahsiv, Pam, Shimura Kanbei, and Uncle Sun.
Shrimp Meat - Sold by Aoi, Butrus, Dongsheng, Hamawi, Hinterman, Obata, Pam, and Uncle Sun.
Ham - Processing. Sold by Benthem, Chanca, Chef Mao, Frith, Katya, Pitot, Sanguinetti, and Sara.
Bacon - Processing. Sold by Benthem, Chanca, Frith, Katya, Lambad, Pitot, Sanguinetti, Sara, and Shimura Kanbei.
Sausage - Processing. Sold by Benthem, Chanca, Chef Mao, Frith, Katya, Lambad, Pitot, Sanguinetti, Sara, Shimura Kanbei, and Verr Goldet.

Wild Gathered

Mushroom - Found in the wild. Sold by Boucicaut, Bounama, and Chef Mao. Reward from Expeditions. Gardening with Jade Field.
Sweet Flower - Found in the wild. Sold by Flora and Richeza. Reward from Expeditions. Gardening with Jade Field.
Carrot - Sold by Chef Mao. Reward from Expeditions. Investigation. Gardening with Jade Field.
Radish - Sold by Chef Mao and Shimura Kanbei. Reward from Expeditions. Investigation. Gardening with Jade Field.
Snapdragon - Found in the wild. Sold by Chloris. Reward from Expeditions. Gardening with Orderly Meadow.
Mint - Found in the wild. Sold by Chloris. Gardening with Jade Field.
Pinecone - Found in the wild. Reward from Expeditions.
Lotus Head - Found near lakes and river banks in Liyue. Sold by Chef Mao and Herbalist Gui. Reward from Expeditions. Gardening with Orderly Meadow.
Jueyun Chili - Found in the wild in Minlin, Qingce Village, and Stone Gate. Sold by Chef Mao and Fengtai. Gardening with Jade Field.
Qingxin - Found in the heights of Liyue, especially Minlin and Wuwang Hill. Sold by Babak and Herbalist Gui. Gardening with Luxuriant Glebe.
Horsetail - Found in Liyue, mainly Dihua Marsh. Sold by Herbalist Gui. Reward from Expeditions. Gardening with Orderly Meadow.
Violetgrass - Found near cliffs in Liyue. Sold by Babak, Fengtai, Herbalist Gui, and Verr Goldet. Gardening with Luxuriant Glebe.
Berry - Found in the wild.
Small Lamp Grass - Found in the wild in Mondstadt, especially Whispering Woods. Sold by Flora. Gardening with Jade Field.
Calla Lily - Found near lakes in Mondstadt, especially Springvale. Sold by Flora. Gardening with Orderly Meadow.
Apple - Found in the wild, dropped from trees, and sometimes sold by Bolai in Liyue Harbor.
Sunsettia - Found on trees throughout Teyvat, especially in Mondstadt and Liyue.
Matsutake - Found in the wild. Sold by Chef Mao and Verr Goldet. Reward from Expeditions.
Bamboo Shoot - Found in the wild. Sold by Chef Mao.
Sakura Bloom - Found in the wild on Narukami Island.
Seagrass - Found in the wild in Inazuma. Sold by Pam and Shimura Kanbei. Reward from Expeditions. Gardening with Orderly Meadow.
Lavender Melon - Found in the wild in Inazuma. Sold by Shimura Kanbei. Reward from Expeditions.
Zaytun Peach - Found in the wild. Sold by Aramani, Azalai, Eugenie, and Hamawi. Gardening with Jade Field. Reward from Expeditions.
Sumeru Rose - Found in the wild. Gardening with Luxuriant Glebe. Reward from Expeditions.
Ajilenakh Nut - Found in the wild. Sold by Azalai and Eugenie.
Marcotte - Found in the wild. Sold by Boucicaut and Eugenie. Reward from Expeditions. Gardening with Jade Field.
Tidalga - Diving. Sold by Hinterman. Reward from Expeditions.
Chenyu Adeptea - Found in the wild in Chenyu Vale, especially in Qiaoying Village and Yilong Wharf. Sold by Fengtai and Lianfang.
Quenepa Berry - Found in Natlan. Sold by Cintli and Kuzmich.
Nocturnal Blossom - Found in the wild. Sold by Kuzmich and Richeza.
Lakkaberry - Found in the wild. No vendor sells Lakkaberry.
Midsommar Berry - Found in the wild. No vendor sells Midsommar Berry.
Windrest Flower - Found in the wild.

Milk - Sold by Aoi, Blanche (Mondstadt), Boucicaut, Bounama, Cairuo, Dongsheng, Hamawi, Kiyoko, Kofitse, and Pitot.

Potato - Investigation. Sold by Aoi, Blanche (Mondstadt), Boucicaut, Bounama, Cairuo, Changshun, Cintli, Dongsheng, Hamawi, and Kiyoko.
Rice - Sold by Aoi, Dongsheng, Fengtai, and Hamawi.
Tofu - Sold by Aoi, Dongsheng, Fengtai, Hamawi, Kiyoko, and Ms. Bai.
Almond - Sold by Boucicaut, Dongsheng, and Ms. Bai.

Flour - Processing. Sold by Azalai, Benthem, Chanca, Chef Mao, Frith, Katya, Lambad, Sanguinetti, Sara, and Shimura Kanbei.
Cream - Sold by Benthem, Chanca, Frith, Katya, Sanguinetti, and Sara. Processing.
Butter - Sold by Benthem, Chanca, Frith, Katya, Rama, Sanguinetti, Sara, and Verr Goldet. Processing.
Sugar - Processing. Sold by Azalai, Benthem, Chanca, Changshun, Chef Mao, Frith, Katya, Lambad, Rama, Sanguinetti, Sara, and Shimura Kanbei.
Jam - Sold by Benthem, Chanca, Frith, Katya, Sanguinetti, and Sara. Processing.
Cheese - Processing. Sold by Benthem, Chanca, Changshun, Frith, Katya, Pitot, Rama, Sanguinetti, and Sara.
Spice - Processing. Sold by Ashpazi, Azalai, Jut, Lambad, and Rama.
Smetana - Processing. No vendor sells Smetana.
Rye Flour - Details not provided in your list.
