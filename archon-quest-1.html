Act I — Of the Land Amidst Monoliths

The Traveler arrives in Liyue during the Rite of Descension and witnesses Rex Lapis’ apparent fall. As the city grapples with fear and suspicion, the Traveler works with Childe, Zhongli, and the Liyue Qixing to uncover the truth behind the Archon’s “death.”

Amid contracts, adepti traditions, and Fatui schemes, the Traveler helps defend the harbor and learns that Zhongli himself is the Geo Archon. He willingly parts with his Gnosis, testing whether Liyue can stand on its own without the Archon’s direct rule.
