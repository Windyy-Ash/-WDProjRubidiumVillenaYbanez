Act III — Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana

In Sumeru, the Traveler is entangled in the Sages’ plan to harvest dreams and control knowledge through the Akademiya. Partnering with Nahida and allies across rainforest and desert, they uncover the truth behind forbidden knowledge and the Scarlet King.

By confronting the God of Wisdom’s confinement and the Sages’ ambitions, the Traveler helps free Nahida and redirects Sumeru toward a more open pursuit of knowledge.
